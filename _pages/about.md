I am a Ph.D. student at **Shenzhen University**.

Before joining Shenzhen University, I received my M.Eng. in Computer Technology from **Changsha University of Science and Technology**, where my research focused on **edge computing, Service Function Chain (SFC) deployment, and VNF deployment and migration**. I later worked as a full-time lecturer at the **Guangzhou College of Applied Science and Technology**.

My current research interests include **embodied AI, intelligent robotics, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models, Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs), FPGA acceleration, and real-time systems**.

You can find more details about my academic background and experience in my **[CV]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/pdf/Wenqiang_Deng_CV.pdf)**.

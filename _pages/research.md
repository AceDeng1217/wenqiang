## Research Interests

My current research interests broadly include:

- **Embodied AI and Intelligent Robotics**
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models**
- **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)**
- **Neuromorphic and Event-Driven Computing**
- **FPGA Acceleration and Real-Time Systems**

I am interested in intelligent robotic systems that combine **high-level perception and decision-making** with **efficient and low-latency physical interaction**. In particular, I am exploring the potential of VLA/VLM-based robotic intelligence, event-driven neural computation, and heterogeneous computing platforms.

## Previous Research

During my master's study at **Changsha University of Science and Technology**, my research focused on **edge computing and network optimization**, particularly Service Function Chain (SFC) deployment and Virtual Network Function (VNF) deployment and migration.

My previous work investigated algorithmic approaches for improving deployment efficiency and reducing network latency in edge computing environments.

This research resulted in publications in **Computer Networks** and **IEEE ISPA 2024**.
